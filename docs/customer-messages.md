# Customer Messages for Exercises

## 1 — Loud tone, low impact
Subject: THIS IS RIDICULOUS!!!
I have been trying for ten minutes to find where I can download last month's receipt. Why is this so difficult? Please send me the receipt or tell me where to find it.

## 2 — Calm wording, possible high risk
Subject: Question about transactions
Hello, I noticed three card transactions in my account this morning that I don't recognise. I still have my card with me. Could you please check this?

## 3 — Medium
Subject: Payment failed
Hi, I tried to pay an invoice this morning but the payment failed. I need to complete it today. Can you tell me what I should do next?

## 4 — Low
Subject: Change company address
Hello, our company moved offices last month. How can I update the billing address shown on future invoices?

## 5 — High
Subject: Possible duplicate charges
Hi, our finance team sees the same €4,850 payment charged three times. Please investigate urgently because this affects today's cash position.

## 6 — Tone trap
Subject: URGENT!!! My logo is wrong!!!
Your portal is showing our old company logo and this is completely unacceptable!!! Fix it NOW please.

## 7 — MCP lookup
Subject: TX-1007
Hello, transaction TX-1007 still doesn't look right in our system. Can you check what happened and tell me whether I need to take action?

## 8 — MCP lookup
Subject: TX-1014
Hi, I don't recognise transaction TX-1014. I haven't shared my card with anyone. Please investigate.

## 9 — MCP lookup
Subject: TX-1018
Hello, could you check TX-1018? The merchant says the payment did not arrive, but I thought it had completed.

## 10 — Missing information
Subject: Payment issue
My payment isn't working. Please fix it.
